# Inflatrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-09

Inflatrack is a personal finance organization app for recording expenses, income, budgets, payment methods, categories, cash boxes, assigned people, and local reports.

## Data Stored By The App

Inflatrack stores the information entered by the user locally on the device, including financial records, categories, subcategories, payment methods, budgets, assigned people, app language, theme, default currency, and biometric lock preference.

Inflatrack does not create user accounts, does not upload financial records to Inflatrack servers, does not use analytics, does not show advertising, and does not sell user information.

## Exports And Sharing

The app can generate CSV and PDF reports. These files are created only when the user starts an export or share action. After a user shares a file, the selected destination app or service handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Authentication

Inflatrack can use device authentication through Android biometric/passcode APIs. Inflatrack does not receive, store, or transmit biometric templates or device passcodes.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Records remain on the device until the user edits/deletes them, clears the app storage, or uninstalls the app. Because the app does not maintain an Inflatrack server account, deleting local app data removes the app's stored records from that device.

## Third Parties

Inflatrack uses platform and Expo/React Native libraries to run the app, store local data, authenticate with the device, print PDF files, and invoke the operating system share sheet. Inflatrack does not intentionally share user-entered financial records with third parties except when the user chooses to export or share a file.
